# 🌙 Kids Night Out — The Playbook (rinse & repeat)

**Owner:** Rhett Morrow · **By:** Jacky · **2026-06-10**
**What it is:** Big Star's recurring community disco. Not an event you remember
to run — a **tradition families count on**. Predictability = belonging =
retention. Every franchise inherits this playbook.

## The rules (locked)
- **Frequency:** 4× a year — **one mid-way through every term.**
- **Time:** ~**5:30–8:30pm** on a **Saturday** near the middle of the term.
- **Price:** **FREE for members** (their weekly fee covers it) · **$60 non-members.**
- **Booking:** **members book first** (priority window), then opens to all.
- **Staffing:** **Rhett + 2–3 coaches**, rostered **~2 weeks before**.
- **Everything is FREE for all participants:** pizza, prizes, glow toys, all activities.
- **On the night:** small **competitions → prizes for all participants** —
  *Best Dancer · Best Dressed · Best Teamwork.* Always a dress-up element.
- **Rhett provides the setup:** DJ table, lights, sound, music.

## 🎨 THEME BANK (each disco a different theme — never repeat)
Each theme = a vibe + 3–4 games + a music feel.

1. **Glow Circus** ✨ (neon/UV, glow sticks, white clothes) — *great for winter
   nights.* Games: glow limbo · freeze-dance · parachute glow-wave · hula-hoop
   contest. Music: upbeat clean pop / Kidz Bop.
2. **Superhero Spectacular** 🦸 (come as your hero) — Games: "save the city"
   obstacle relay · freeze-dance "statues of power" · cape parade · tug-of-war.
   Music: movie-hero anthems + party pop.
3. **Tropical Beach Party / Luau** 🌴 (leis, Hawaiian shirts) — Games: limbo ·
   beach-ball keep-up · conga line · musical islands. Music: summery party hits.
4. **Space / Galaxy Disco** 🚀 — Games: "asteroid dodge" (balloon stomp) ·
   freeze-dance · planet hula-hoops · parachute "rocket launch". Music: spacey pop.
5. **Spooky Circus** 🎃 (great for a term near Halloween) — Games: monster-mash
   dance-off · mummy-wrap relay · musical statues. Music: Halloween party mix.
6. **Under the Big Top Carnival** 🎪 — circus-skill stations (mini-trapeze,
   tightrope line, juggling scarves) + dance breaks. Music: big-top + pop.
7. **PJ Disco** 🌙 · 8. **Wild West Rodeo** 🤠 · 9. **Retro Disco Fever** 🕺 ·
   10. **Winter Wonderland / Christmas** ❄️ (end-of-year Term 4).
