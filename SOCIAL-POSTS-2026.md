### Tips
- **Reels > photos** for reach: a 10–15s clip of a child nailing a trick will always
  outperform a still. Same captions work.
- Post **Star of the Month** as a carousel (photo + a fun fact) — highest shares from mums/grandmas.
- Always end with the **phone number or "link in bio"** — make booking one tap away.
- Reshare any tagged parent photos to your Story — free social proof.
