# 📬 Big Star Circus — 2026 Monthly Newsletters (ready to send)

**By:** Jacky · **For:** Rhett Morrow
**How to use:** One newsletter per month. Paste into the CRM Campaigns editor (drag-drop
blocks), drop in a hero photo + the student spotlight, check the dates, send. Each follows
the proven structure below (modelled on Flipside Circus + Circa, fitted to mums/grandmas).

**The structure every month (keep it consistent):**
1. Hero photo + warm one-line greeting
2. Welcome note from Jacky/the team
3. **This month at Big Star** (the #1 thing) + red CTA
4. Classes open + **"Book a Free Trial"** (hero CTA — our biggest lever)
5. ⭐ **Star of the Month** (a child's photo + a win — mums/grandmas share these)
6. 📅 Term dates / holiday program box
7. Behind the scenes / community moment
8. What's on around the Gold Coast (from Events Radar)
9. Closing CTA — "Book a Free Trial" again
10. Footer: 0489 188 179 · admin@bigstarcircus.com.au · Molendinar · socials

**Brand:** red #D72027 (buttons/headers), gold #FFC107 (badges/accents), lots of white space,
one big joyful photo up top. Short paragraphs — grandmas read on phones.

**Evergreen CTAs to reuse:** `Book a Free Trial` · `Enrol Now` · `Play On Vouchers welcome`
