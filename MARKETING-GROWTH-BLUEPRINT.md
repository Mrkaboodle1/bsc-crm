## 1. 🎯 THE AUDIENCE BOARD — who we market to

### Primary: "Circus Mum"
- **Age:** 30–45. The decision-maker and the booker.
- **Mindset:** wants her kid active, confident, off screens, *and* something
  she can be proud of / show off (photos, milestones, "look what my kid did").
- **Buys on:** safety, belonging, her child thriving, easy/convenient.

### Secondary (surprisingly strong): "Spoiling Grandmother"
- **Age:** 50–58. Booking grandkids into something **different & enriching**.
- **Mindset:** wants quality time + a special experience; **willing to spend.**
- **Buys on:** uniqueness ("not just another sport"), the joy on the kid's face.

### Geography
- **~5 km radius of Molendinar / Gold Coast.** Tight, local, high-intent.

### Where they are (channel priority — from what's actually worked)
1. **Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads** — your proven #1 source of enquiries.
2. **Instagram + Facebook organic** (3×/week presence).
3. **YouTube** (Rhett Star) — trust & warmth.
4. **Word-of-mouth / referrals** — your warmest leads.

> Every ad, post, email and offer is aimed at these two people, in this radius.

## 2. 🛤️ THE PATHWAY — the journey + where automation fires

| Stage | What happens | Automation (draft → approve → send) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Discover** | Sees us on FB/IG/YouTube or word-of-mouth | 3×/week social posts; Meta ads (replacing Joe) |
| **2. Enquire** | Fills the trial/enquiry form → becomes a *lead* | ✅ LIVE: instant welcome email (Hive) |
| **3. Trial** | Books & attends a free trial | Reminder (day before) + "how was it?" (day after) |
| **4. Join** | Becomes a paying member | Welcome/onboarding: what to bring, wristband, portal |
| **5. Engage & Retain** | Comes weekly, hits milestones, hears about events | Monthly newsletter; event invites (KNO, holiday prog); milestone & "we missed you" texts |
| **6. Refer** | Brings a friend / leaves a review | Referral ask after a win; review request |

**Key insight:** retention = *make them feel seen* (milestones, photos, events).
Events (KNO, holiday workshops, shows) are the heartbeat of retention — but they
only work if the **calendar is filled with real dates** (see below).
