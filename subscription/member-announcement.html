Big changes at Big Star Circus

Bigger, better — all year round. Starting Term 4 (Mon 6 Oct).

Hi there,

We've been building something special, and from Term 4 (Monday 6 October) Big Star Circus is leveling up for your family. Here's what's coming — and why.

What's changing

We're moving to a simple all-year membership — $30 a week for your child's weekly class, billed every week of the year (no more stop-start each term). Why all year? Because we're now giving you more, all year:

  • 🎪 Classes run year-round — even through public holidays.
  • 📱 The brand-new BigStar Kids parent app — track your child's skills, stars, coach praise, photos and progress on your phone.
  • ⭐ A StarBand wristband + Star Reward sheets for every member.
  • 🏕️ School-holiday workshops included differently (see below) — huge value.
  • 🎉 Kids Night Out at half price.

✅ Your membership includes more than classes. The school-holiday program and Kids Night Out are part of your $30 all-year subscription — member benefits, not separate sign-ups. Here's how they work:

🏕️ School Holiday Program — included in your membership

Your all-year membership includes the school-holiday program. During school holidays your normal class doesn't run — instead, on your child's usual class day we run a FULL workshop, 9am–3pm (6 hours), and you still only pay your usual $30 (it's already in your subscription). That's six hours of circus for the price of one!
  • 📅 Members book first. Each term we open holiday-workshop bookings to members for about 5 weeks (we'll text you the exact dates). Book in your window to lock in your spot — after it closes, bookings open to the public and spots can fill, so don't miss out.
  • Your member rate applies to your regular class day's workshop only. Can't make that day? You're welcome to book another day — but it's $60 (member days can't be swapped), and extra days are $60 each too.
  • Away and miss it? You'll still pay that week's subscription (your usual $30) — it's an all-year membership, so weeks aren't cancelled or refunded.
  • Non-members pay $60 per day.

🎉 Kids Night Out — included in your membership

Kids Night Out is included free for members — your weekly fee covers it (non-members pay $60). It runs 4 times a year, once per term. Totally optional, always a blast. Members book first here too — get in early before spots open to the public.

🛡️ A note on safety — sign-in & sign-out

From Term 4 we're introducing sign-in and sign-out for every child — a quick tap of their StarBand wristband or a login on our iPad, alongside the coaches' roll. This is how we're strengthening our duty of care, so we always know exactly who's in our care and who collects them. It reflects the safe-environment practice expected under Queensland's new Child Safe Standards — and it gives every parent peace of mind. Learn more at qfcc.qld.gov.au/childsafe.

A couple of notes

  • Missed a regular class? You can do up to 2 make-up classes per term (same as always) — just let us know in advance and we'll fit you into a suitable class that term.
  • Cancellations need 3 weeks' notice (up from 2) — fair for an all-year plan.
  • Nothing changes until Term 4 (6 Oct). This is your heads-up.

We're so excited for this next chapter — it's all about giving your kids more circus, more progress, and a safer, brighter Big Star. 🎪
