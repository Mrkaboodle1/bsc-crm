## 📣 The announcement (email + survey intro)

**Subject:** Big changes at Big Star Circus — bigger, better, all year ⭐

Hi {first_name},

We've been building something special, and from **Term 4 (Monday 6 October)**
Big Star Circus is leveling up for your family. Here's what's coming — and why.

### What's changing
We're moving to a simple **all-year membership** — **$30 a week for your child's
weekly class**, billed every week of the year (no more stop-start each term).

Why all year? Because we're now giving you *more*, all year:

- 🎪 **Classes run year-round** — even through public holidays. More circus, no gaps.
- 📱 **The brand-new BigStar Kids parent app** — track your child's skills, stars,
  praise from coaches, photos and progress, right on your phone.
- ⭐ **A StarBand wristband** + Star Reward sheets for every member.
- 🏕️ **School-holiday workshops included differently** (see below) — huge value.
- 🎉 **Kids Night Out at half price.**

### Your membership — what's included
| | |
|---|---|
| 1 class / week (1st child) | **$30/week** |
| 2 classes / week ($25 + $25) | **$50/week** |
| 3 classes / week ($20 + $20 + $20) | **$60/week** |
| Sibling (per child) — $20 per class | **$20 / $40 / $60** for 1 / 2 / 3 classes |

*All prices are **GST-free** — the price you see is the price you pay, no tax added.*

**✅ Your membership includes more than classes** — the **School Holiday Program**
and **Kids Night Out** are **part of your $30 all-year subscription** (member
benefits, not separate sign-ups). Here's how they work:

### 🏕️ School Holiday Program — included in your membership
Your all-year membership **includes the school-holiday program**. During school
holidays your normal class doesn't run — instead, **on your child's usual class
day we run a FULL workshop, 9am–3pm (6 hours)** — and you still **only pay your
usual $30** (it's already in your subscription). That's **six hours of circus for
the price of one** (a workshop day is normally $60).

- 📅 **Members book first.** Each term we open holiday-workshop bookings to
  members for about **5 weeks** (we'll text you the exact dates). Book in your
  window to lock in your spot — **after it closes, bookings open to the public
  and spots can fill, so don't miss out.**
- Your member rate applies to **your regular class day's workshop only**. Can't
  make that day? You're welcome to book another day — but it's **$60** (member
  days can't be swapped), and extra days are **$60 each** too.
- Away and miss it? **You'll still pay that week's subscription (your usual $30)** —
  it's an all-year membership, so weeks aren't cancelled or refunded.
- Non-members pay **$60 per day** for workshops.

### 🎉 Kids Night Out — included in your membership
Kids Night Out is **included free** for members — your weekly fee covers it
(non-members pay $60). It runs **4 times a year, once per term**. Totally
optional, always a blast. **Members book first here too** — get in early before
spots open to the public.

### 🛡️ A note on safety — sign-in & sign-out (please read)
From Term 4 we're introducing **sign-in and sign-out for every child** — a quick
tap of their **StarBand wristband** or a login on our iPad, alongside the
coaches' roll. This is how we're strengthening our **duty of care**, so we
always know exactly who's in our care and who collects them. It reflects the
safe-environment practice expected under **Queensland's new Child Safe
Standards** — and it gives every parent peace of mind.
Learn more: qfcc.qld.gov.au/childsafe

### A couple of housekeeping notes
- **Missed a regular class?** You can do up to **2 make-up classes per term**
  (same as always) — just let us know in advance and we'll fit you into a
  suitable class that term.
- Cancellations need **3 weeks' notice** (up from 2) — fair for an all-year plan.
- **Nothing changes until Term 4 (6 Oct).** This is your heads-up.
