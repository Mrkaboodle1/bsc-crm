New Membership — We'd Love Your Feedback

From Term 4 (Mon 6 Oct) we're introducing a new all-year membership. Before we roll it out, please read the summary and tell us what you think.

Quick summary — what's coming

  • All-year membership, billed weekly across the whole year (incl. school & public holidays).
  • Pricing (GST-free): 1st child $30 / $50 / $60 for 1 / 2 / 3 classes a week · siblings $20 / $40 / $60.
  • School Holiday Program included — on your child's usual class day, a full 6-hour workshop (9am–3pm) for your normal weekly fee. Members book first. Extra/other days $60.
  • Kids Night Out included (4× a year) — free as part of your membership. Members book first.
  • BigStar Kids / Parent app + StarBand wristband — track skills, stars, coach feedback, photos; quick sign-in/out.
  • Safer check-in — every child signed in & out (wristband or digital), aligned with QLD Child Safe Standards.
  • Up to 2 make-up classes per term · cancellations need 3 weeks' notice · nothing changes until 6 Oct.
