How parents, staff and children raise concerns — and how we respond, every time.

Complaint Handling

1. Our commitment

BigStar Circus takes every complaint seriously. We respond promptly, fairly, and without retaliation. We treat the complainant with respect, maintain confidentiality, and document every step.

2. Who can complain

  • Children — via a coach they trust, the Director directly, or a parent.
  • Parents and guardians.
  • Staff and contractors.
  • Visitors and community members.
  • Anonymous complainants — via the form on the BSC website.

4. Child-friendly reporting

  • Every classroom has a "Tell a coach" card at child-eye level showing the names and photos of the team and how to ask for help.
  • Coaches ask "Anything you want to tell me?" at the end of each class as a normal, low-pressure prompt.
  • Children can write or draw a concern and put it in the orange "Tell Rhett" box at the front of the studio.

5. Step-by-step flow

  1. Receive. Whoever first receives the complaint records the date, time, channel, and the words used — verbatim where possible — in the BSC CRM Complaint log.
  2. Acknowledge. Within 1 working day the complainant is acknowledged personally by Director Rhett (in writing or by phone).
  3. Triage. Director decides: minor service issue, formal complaint, or child-safety concern. Child-safety concerns escalate immediately to the Reporting Flowchart (Doc BSC-CS-09) and may trigger a mandatory external report.
  4. Investigate. Gather facts: speak with all parties, review CRM records, check CCTV if relevant, consult Lead Coach. Investigation completed within 10 working days. For complex cases an extension is communicated to the complainant.
  5. Decide. Director documents findings, decision and reasons in the BSC CRM.
  6. Respond. Complainant is told the outcome in writing — what was found, what action is being taken, and how to escalate further if not satisfied.
  7. Review. Lessons-learned step added to the next quarterly Risk Management review.

6. Escalation

If a complainant is not satisfied with the BSC response, they can escalate to:

  • Office of Fair Trading Queensland — for service-related consumer matters.
  • QFCC — for child safety standards complaints.
  • Queensland Police — for criminal matters.
  • Office of the Information Commissioner — for privacy matters.

7. Confidentiality

Information is shared only on a need-to-know basis. The complainant’s identity is protected as far as the law allows. Mandatory reports to QPS or QFCC over-ride confidentiality and the complainant is told this upfront.

8. No retaliation

No one who raises a complaint in good faith will be subject to disciplinary action, reduced access, social pressure, or any other form of retaliation. Retaliation is itself a Code of Conduct breach (Doc BSC-CS-04).

9. Record keeping

Every complaint — including anonymous ones — is recorded in the BSC CRM with: date, channel, summary, triage decision, investigation steps, outcome, complainant notification, lessons learned. Records held for 7 years.

Approved: Rhett Morrow, Director & Child Safety Officer — 27 May 2026.
