⚠️ Read first — not legal advice. In Queensland a restraint is presumed void unless the employer proves it is reasonably necessary to protect a genuine business interest. Courts judge it on time, distance and scope. From 2027, non-compete clauses are expected to be banned for employees earning under ~$183,100 (which includes coaches) — so treat the "can't work for a rival" part as a deterrent/backup, and rely on the non-solicitation part as your real protection. Have an employment lawyer review before anyone signs.

1. What we're protecting (legitimate interests)

BSC has a genuine interest in protecting its student & family relationships, its staff, its confidential curriculum, methods and pricing, and the specialist training it invests in coaches. This restraint exists only to protect those interests — not to stop fair competition.

2. Restraint period & area (cascading)

The restraints below apply for the longest period and widest area that a court finds reasonable, working down this ladder. Each period and each distance is a separate, severable obligation:

3. Non-solicitation (the strong part)

During the restraint period the Coach must not, directly or indirectly:

  1. approach, canvass or solicit any BSC student or family (that they dealt with in their last 12 months) to leave BSC or join a competing circus/aerial/gymnastics school;
  2. approach or solicit any BSC coach, trainee or staff member to leave BSC; or
  3. use or disclose BSC's confidential information (curriculum, lesson plans, student lists, pricing).

4. Non-compete (deterrent / backup)

During the restraint period and within the restraint area, the Coach must not own, operate, or be employed by a business that directly competes with BSC's children's circus, aerial and gymnastics classes. (This is the part most likely to be limited by law — see the warning above.)

5. Reasonableness & severance

The parties agree each restraint is reasonable and goes no further than needed to protect BSC's interests. If any period, area or activity is found unenforceable, it is severed and the remaining combinations continue to apply to the fullest extent the law allows.

6. Acknowledgement

The Coach confirms they have read this restraint, had the opportunity to get their own legal advice, received valuable consideration (including paid training), and agree to it freely.
