Parent Code of Conduct

Welcome — and thank you

Thank you for trusting BigStar Circus with your child. This short guide sets out a few simple expectations that keep the studio safe, fair, and a place every kid wants to come back to.

1. Respect

  • Treat coaches, staff, other parents and other children with respect at all times — in the studio, in the carpark, and online.
  • Disagreements happen. Raise them with the Director privately, not in front of children.
  • BSC is a welcoming, inclusive space. Discriminatory comments about race, religion, gender identity, ability, or family structure are not tolerated.

2. Pickup & Dropoff

  • Drop children at the studio door — not in the carpark from a moving car.
  • Be on time. Late pickup leaves a child waiting with one coach holding the room together.
  • Only adults on your Authorised Collectors list (managed in the BSC CRM) may collect your child. Tell us in advance if someone different is collecting.
  • For children under 7, sign them in and out at the office.

3. Photography & Privacy

  • You may photograph your own child. You may not photograph other people’s children without their parent’s explicit permission.
  • Group photos posted to social media should not tag, name, or identify other people’s children.
  • BigStar Circus only posts photos of children whose parents have ticked Media Consent in the CRM.
  • No photography or filming inside change-room areas.

4. Social Media

  • Comments about BigStar Circus, our coaches or other families on public platforms should be honest, respectful, and constructive.
  • Public complaints about BSC are handled through our Complaint Handling Process (Doc BSC-CS-08) — not via tagged Instagram posts or Facebook group rants. We will always answer a direct, private complaint within two working days.
  • Do not friend or follow other families’ children from your personal account if you do not know them outside BSC.

5. Behaviour Expectations

  • Coaching the class is the coach’s job. Please do not call out instructions, corrections, or criticisms from the side of the room.
  • If your child is having a hard day, talk to the coach quietly — we’ll work it out together.
  • Younger siblings are welcome to wait quietly with a parent, but cannot run around the studio floor while a class is in session.
  • No food or drink (other than water) on the studio floor.

6. Communication

The best ways to reach BigStar Circus:

  • Email: admin@bigstarcircus.com.au
  • Phone / SMS — Admin Jackie: 0439 188 179
  • Phone — Director Rhett (urgent or child-safety only): 0437 186 533
  • Anonymous complaint: form on the BigStar Circus website

We aim to reply within one working day. Mass-emails and SMS replies are managed through the BSC CRM Inbox for accountability.

7. Child Safety Reporting

If you have a concern about a child’s safety — yours or another child’s — tell us immediately. Call Rhett directly on 0437 186 533. If you believe a child is in immediate danger, call 000. You can also report directly to Queensland Child Safety on 1300 682 254 (business hours) or 1800 811 810 (after hours).

BigStar Circus will take every concern seriously, investigate thoroughly, and where required by law, report to the authorities. Confidentiality is maintained as far as the law allows.
