Employment law is changing: the Australian Government has announced a ban on non-compete clauses for most workers (from 2027), and courts already rarely enforce restraints against lower-paid staff. The Training Repayment section below is the strongest, fairest part. Have an employment lawyer review and tailor this before you ask anyone to sign it.

1. Why this agreement exists

BSC will invest significant time and money training the Coach in specialist circus, aerial and safety skills — including paid training hours, courses, certifications and mentoring. This Agreement makes sure that investment is fair to both sides.

2. Training Repayment (the training bond)

If the Coach resigns, or is dismissed for serious misconduct, within 24 months of completing BSC-funded training, the Coach agrees to repay a share of the genuine, documented cost of that training, on this reducing scale:

If the Coach leaves…Repayment
Within 6 months of training100% of documented cost
6–12 months75%
12–18 months50%
18–24 months25%
After 24 monthsNil — fully earned

The repayable amount reflects only actual, receipted costs (course fees, certifications, paid training time), recorded in the Coach's Training Logbook, and is capped at $ . It is a genuine recovery of cost, not a penalty. Where lawfully permitted and agreed in writing, BSC may deduct it from final pay; otherwise BSC will invoice the Coach.

This does not apply if BSC ends the employment for any reason other than the Coach's serious misconduct, or if the Coach leaves due to a genuine BSC breach.

3. Confidentiality & intellectual property

During and after employment, the Coach will keep confidential — and will not use for any other business — BSC's curriculum, lesson plans, training methods, Star Reward system, pricing, and any student or family information. All BSC training materials and systems remain the property of BSC.

4. Reasonable restraint (non-solicitation)

For 6 months after leaving BSC, the Coach agrees not to:

  1. approach or solicit BSC students or families to leave BSC for a competing circus/aerial school;
  2. approach or solicit BSC staff to leave; and
  3. start, or work for, a directly competing children's circus/aerial school within 5 km of the BSC studio.

Each of the above applies separately. If any part is found to be unreasonable or unenforceable, the rest still apply to the extent the law allows. (Note: restraint 3 is the part most likely to be limited by the law — restraints 1 & 2, protecting our students and staff, are the most defensible.)

5. Acknowledgement

The Coach confirms they have read this Agreement, had the chance to seek their own advice, and agree to it freely.
