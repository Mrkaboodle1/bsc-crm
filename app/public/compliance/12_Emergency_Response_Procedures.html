Emergency Procedures

1. Emergency Exits & Equipment

Primary emergency exit: rear roller door opening directly onto the carpark.
Secondary exit: front office door onto Harper Street.
Both routes are kept clear of equipment, bags, and mats at all times. Lead Coach checks before every class.

Assembly point: front edge of the BSC carpark, well clear of the building, away from Harper Street traffic.

Drills: a full evacuation drill is run once per month with the team and once per term with students in attendance.

2. Injury Response

  1. Stop the class. Lead Coach calls "PAUSE" — all students freeze.
  2. Secure the area. Move other students back. Get the injured child onto a safe spot if possible.
  3. Apply first aid. The closest HLTAID011-certified coach takes over. Gloves on if there’s blood.
  4. Assess severity.
    • Minor (scrape, mild knock): apply, monitor, complete Incident Report.
    • Moderate (sprain, head bump, breathing difficulty): take aside, monitor closely, call parent.
    • Severe (suspected fracture, unconsciousness, anaphylaxis, severe bleeding): call 000 immediately. Stay with the child. Send another adult to meet the ambulance at Harper St.
  5. Notify parent. Director or Admin makes the call within minutes for moderate / severe; same day for minor.
  6. Document. Complete Incident Report Form (Doc BSC-CS-07) within 24 hours.

3. Fire / Evacuation

  1. Lead Coach calls "EVACUATE NOW".
  2. Students line up calmly — tallest at the front, smallest at the back of the line (so the coach at the back can see everyone).
  3. Walk — do not run — through the primary exit (rear roller door). If blocked, divert to the secondary exit (front office door).
  4. Coach picks up the day’s Roll Call iPad or printed roll on the way out.
  5. Assemble at the front edge of the carpark. Coach counts every child against the roll and confirms with the Lead Coach.
  6. Lead Coach phones 000 if not already called.
  7. No one re-enters the building until the fire service or Lead Coach gives the all-clear.
  8. Parents collecting are directed to the assembly point only.

4. Missing Child Procedure

Triggered the moment a coach realises a child who was present at the last head count is not visible.

  1. Inform the Lead Coach immediately.
  2. Pair search (5 minutes maximum): two staff sweep the main studio, change rooms, toilets, office, then the carpark and Harper Street frontage.
  3. Other classes continue under remaining coaches.
  4. If not found within 5 minutes:
    • Phone the parent — calm, factual.
    • Phone Queensland Police — 000 or 131 444.
    • One staff member stays at the studio in case the child returns; one walks the immediate streets.
  5. Document everything: times, who searched where, who was contacted when. Incident Report submitted regardless of outcome.

5. Medical Emergencies (asthma, anaphylaxis, seizure, diabetic)

  • Medical alerts are flagged against each student in the BSC CRM at sign-up. Lead Coach checks alerts before every class.
  • EpiPens / asthma plans / inhalers travel with the parent during class where appropriate, OR are kept in a labelled medical box accessible to all staff.
  • If a child uses a medication during class, the Coach calls the parent and completes an Incident Report.
  • For anaphylaxis / loss of consciousness / severe asthma: call 000 immediately, administer EpiPen / commence CPR as trained.

6. Lockdown

Triggered by an external threat: an aggressive non-parent on the premises, weapon sighted, police instruction.

  1. Lead Coach calls "LOCKDOWN".
  2. All children moved to the back-of-studio mat zone, away from front windows. Lights dimmed.
  3. Front office door and rear roller door locked. Internal door to the office closed.
  4. Coaches keep children quiet and calm; one coach phones 000.
  5. No movement, no door-opening until Queensland Police arrive and give the all-clear.

7. Severe Weather

  • BoM warnings within 60 km of Molendinar are checked the morning of any outdoor / offsite session.
  • If a severe weather warning is current: outdoor and offsite sessions are cancelled or moved indoors.
  • During a class, if conditions deteriorate (hail, severe wind), classes are paused and children kept indoors, away from windows and the roller door.
  • Parents are SMS’d via the CRM if pickup will be affected.
