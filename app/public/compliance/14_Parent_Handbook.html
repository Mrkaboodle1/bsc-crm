Welcome to BigStar Circus

You’ve just signed your kid up for something a bit different — a real working circus school where they’ll learn aerials, acrobatics, juggling and apparatus from coaches who actually perform for a living. We’re thrilled to have you. This handbook tells you how it all works.

Our mission: Create opportunities for young people to move, connect, and believe in themselves.

1. Class Procedures

1.1 What to bring

  • Water bottle, named.
  • Activewear that allows full movement. No belts, jewellery, or zips that could catch on apparatus.
  • Hair tied back if it falls past the shoulders.
  • Bare feet or grip socks — not regular socks.

1.2 Arrival & dropoff

  • Arrive 5 minutes before class. Walk your child to the studio door.
  • Sign in at the office for any child under 7.
  • Drop on the studio floor — not in the carpark from a moving car.

1.3 Pickup

  • Be on time. Late pickup leaves a child alone with one coach holding the room together.
  • Only adults on your Authorised Collectors list can collect your child. Update the list in the BSC CRM, or tell us by SMS if a different adult is picking up.
  • For under-7s, sign out at the office.

1.4 If your child can’t make it

  • SMS Jackie on 0439 188 179 or reply to your class reminder.
  • Make-up classes can be requested in writing — subject to availability and at the Director’s discretion.
  • If your child is sick — keep them home. We’ll see them next week.

2. Safety at BigStar Circus

Safety is the absolute foundation of everything we do. The systems behind the scenes:

  • Blue Cards. Every coach holds a current Queensland Blue Card, verified directly with the QFCC before their first shift.
  • First Aid. Every shift includes at least one HLTAID011 first-aider with current CPR.
  • Rigging. Every aerial point is inspected by a qualified Lead Coach before every class.
  • Supervision. Coaches are never alone in a closed room with a child. Doors stay open. Two cameras cover the main studio at all times.
  • Pickup security. Children are only released to adults on your authorised list.
  • Insurance. BigStar Circus carries full Public Liability and Professional Indemnity cover. Certificate of Currency available on request.

4. Behaviour Expectations

The full Parent Code of Conduct is BSC-CS-05. In short:

  • Respect coaches, staff, other parents and other children.
  • Coaching is the coach’s job — please don’t call out instructions from the side.
  • No photography of other people’s children without permission.
  • Concerns get raised privately with Rhett, not on social media.

5. Star Reward System

We recognise effort, bravery, kindness and persistence — not just talent. Coaches award Stars during class for things like trying a new skill, helping a friend, or coming back after a tough week. Stars are logged in the BSC CRM. At 10 / 25 / 50 Stars your child unlocks Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers — with a printed certificate and a moment in the spotlight at the next showcase.

6. Showcases, Holidays and Special Events

  • End-of-term showcase — every Term 2 and Term 4. Kids choose their act and music within safety guidelines.
  • School holiday workshops — Term breaks. Bookings open ~4 weeks before each holiday.
  • Kids Night Out — periodic Saturday-night drop-off events. Bookings via the CRM.
  • Birthday parties — on Sundays at the studio. Book via admin@bigstarcircus.com.au.

7. Pricing & Payments

  • Term subscription — the standard weekly billing for ongoing students.
  • Casual — pay-as-you-go for the occasional class.
  • Play On voucher — we accept Queensland Government $200 Play On vouchers. Apply at qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/playon/apply.
  • NDIS — we work with plan-managed, self-managed and agency-managed participants. Available for 1-on-1 sessions on Thursdays and Fridays 9:30am–3:30pm.

All invoices and Stripe receipts are sent from the BSC CRM. Payment queries: admin@bigstarcircus.com.au.

8. Child Safety — how you can help

If you’re worried about any child’s safety — yours or someone else’s — tell us straight away. Phone Rhett on 0437 186 533. If a child is in immediate danger, call 000. You can also report directly to Queensland Child Safety on 1300 682 254 (business hours) or 1800 811 810 (after hours).

BigStar Circus takes every concern seriously. We’ll investigate, document, and where required by law, report to authorities. We’ll never punish or retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
