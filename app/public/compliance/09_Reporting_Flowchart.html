Reporting Flowchart

If you suspect harm to a child — follow this flow

1Is the child in immediate danger?
If yes — call 000 right now. Stay with the child. Do not contact the suspected person.
▼
2Make the child safe.
Separate from the person of concern. Stay with another adult present. Don’t interrogate — listen, write down their exact words afterwards.
▼
3Tell the Director the same day.
Rhett Morrow — 0437 186 533 (call or SMS, any time). If Rhett is the person of concern, escalate directly to QFCC and QPS.
▼
4Make the mandatory report.
  • Child Safety Services (Qld) — business hours: 1300 682 254
  • Child Safety After Hours Service Centre — after hours: 1800 811 810
  • Queensland Police — 000 (emergency) or 131 444 (non-emergency)
  • QFCC — www.qfcc.qld.gov.au
Internal investigation never replaces a mandatory report.
▼
5Document.
Complete the Incident Report Form (Doc BSC-CS-07) and log it in the BSC CRM Incident module within 24 hours. Attach all related evidence.
▼
6Follow up.
The Director or Child Safety Officer follows up with the child’s family (where appropriate and safe to do so), the reporting authorities, and the staff member if relevant.
▼
7Confidentiality and care.
The child is supported through the process. The reporter is thanked, protected from retaliation, and kept informed at appropriate intervals. Records are retained for 7 years minimum.
