BIG STAR TV — MUSIC LIBRARY
===========================

Drop royalty-free music files (.mp3) in THIS folder. The engine picks one per clip.

*** NAME THE FILES DESCRIPTIVELY — THIS IS HOW THE "MUSIC VIBE" BOX WORKS ***

When you type a vibe in the CRM (e.g. "energetic funny circus"), the engine matches
those words against the FILENAMES. So name tracks with the words you'd search for:

  GOOD:  energetic-upbeat-fun-pop.mp3
         funny-quirky-playful-circus.mp3
         happy-uplifting-kids-cheerful.mp3
         epic-inspiring-achievement-triumph.mp3
         calm-gentle-emotional-proud.mp3

  BAD:   track1.mp3   AudioTrack_final_v2.mp3   download (3).mp3

WHERE TO GET FREE, SAFE-TO-POST MUSIC (no copyright strikes):
  1. Pixabay Music          https://pixabay.com/music/   (free, no attribution needed)
  2. YouTube Audio Library  https://studio.youtube.com  -> Audio Library  (free)
  3. Uppbeat               https://uppbeat.io/          (free tier)
  4. Free Music Archive     https://freemusicarchive.org/

Download 5-10 tracks covering these moods and name them as above:
  energetic / funny / happy / epic / calm

DO NOT put commercial music here (Afro Circus, chart songs, etc.) - those will get
your posts muted or struck on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

If this folder is EMPTY, clips keep your original class audio - always safe.
