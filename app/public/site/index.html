About Us

At Bigstar Circus, our vision is to ignite the spark of imagination in every individual, empowering them to dream, believe, and achieve extraordinary feats. Our team of seasoned professionals, deeply rooted in the circus world, are committed to nurturing the skills needed for a stellar journey in the performing arts. Nestled in the lively northern end of the Gold Coast, Bigstar Circus has become the go-to destination for those seeking an innovative and exhilarating circus education.

Rhett Morrow — Head Coach

Born into a family that owned a theatre entertainment restaurant, he grew up amidst the glam and glitter of various forms of Performing Arts. However, it was the thrill and excitement of the circus that truly captured his attention. As a professional circus artist with over 15 years of experience, he has performed locally in Australia and overseas, securing victories at the Australian Street Entertainment Championships in 2015 and 2017. Trained at the renowned International Circus School Beijing, he has been part of various productions such as "Aqua Voyage" in Ocean Park Hong Kong and "The Jungle Book" in Singapore and on Carnival Cruise Line and P&O Cruises.

More about our classes

Unlock Confidence Through Play

At Big Star Circus, kids learn more than just circus tricks – they build self-confidence! Through fun, interactive exercises like juggling, acrobatics, and aerial silks, children are encouraged to step out of their comfort zones in a supportive environment, discovering the joy of achieving new skills.

Nurture Creativity and Teamwork

Our classes are designed to spark creativity and foster collaboration. Children work together to perform routines, develop problem-solving skills, and communicate effectively. Big Star Circus teaches kids how to think on their feet and work as a team, essential skills for life beyond the circus ring!

Strength, Balance & Coordination

Every movement, flip, and swing helps develop strength, balance, and coordination. Our expert instructors guide kids through safe, progressive activities that improve physical fitness and encourage a healthy lifestyle. At Big Star Circus, fitness becomes an exciting adventure!
