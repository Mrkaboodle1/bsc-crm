## 2. Engagement & pay — how it actually works (plain English)

You're bringing coaches on as **independent contractors**, but still doing right by them:

- **They have an ABN** and send you a simple invoice for hours worked.
- **You pay them regularly** (you run it through your pay process so it's reliable).
- **You pay their super on top.** ⚠️ Important and correct: under ATO rules, an
  individual contractor paid mainly for their *labour* is usually entitled to super
  anyway — so paying it is both fair and compliant.
- Keep it clean: a signed contractor agreement + their invoices + super records.

> 🔎 Worth a 10-min chat with your accountant to confirm the contractor-vs-employee
> line for your situation (hours, control, equipment). I've written the ad and
> agreements to fit the contractor + super model you want.

## 3. Interview — keep it simple

**The one question that matters most:**
> "Tell me about a time a child in your class was having a hard day — what did you do?"
(You're listening for patience, warmth, and putting the kid first — not skills.)

**Also ask:**
- Why coaching kids (not performing)?
- Are you reliable for the same class, same time, every week — long term?
- How do you handle an energetic or neurodiverse child who won't listen?
- Do you have / can you get a Blue Card and First Aid?

**🚩 Red flags:** treats coaching as a gap-filler between gigs; vague about reliability;
talks about themselves more than the kids; no Blue Card and no urgency to get one.

**By end of month 1**
- [ ] Co-leads a class, then leads with Rhett watching
- [ ] First training milestone logged (ties to the Training Agreement)
- [ ] Confirm fit + the path: Trainee → Coach → Lead → Head Coach

## 5. The legal docs (already built, in Compliance)
- **Doc 16 — Coach Training Agreement** (`/compliance` → Coach & HR Agreements):
  if BSC invests in their paid training and they leave early, an agreed portion is repaid.
- **Doc 17 — Non-Compete / Restraint of Trade**: protects BSC's families/IP if a coach
  leaves and tries to take clients. (Note: pure non-competes are being restricted in
  Australia from 2027 for lower-paid workers — the **training repayment agreement is the
  stronger, safer protection**. Use both; lean on the training one.)
- **Doc 11 — Blue Card & Training Register**: where you log Blue Cards + First Aid expiries.
