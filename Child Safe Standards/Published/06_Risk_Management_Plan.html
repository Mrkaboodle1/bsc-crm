Child Safe Risk Management Plan

The hazards we’ve identified, the controls we’ve put in place, and the review cycle that keeps them honest.

BigStar Circus Pty Ltd · 14 Harper Street, Molendinar QLD 4214
Version 1.0 · Issued 27 May 2026

Risk Management Plan

1. Purpose

This plan identifies the foreseeable child-safety risks at BigStar Circus and the controls used to manage them. It is a living document — updated after every reportable incident and reviewed in full annually.

2. Risk Register

Risk rating: L (likelihood) × C (consequence), each 1–5. Residual risk shown after controls applied.

Risk areaHazardControlResidual
Physical environmentTrip hazards on studio floor (cables, mats, gear)Daily pre-class sweep by Lead Coach. Equipment returned to allocated zone after use. Cable covers on mains runs.Low
Blind spots in the studio (behind office, change room corridor)Two CCTV cameras in main studio. Coach walks the perimeter at the start of each class.Low
Toilet / change-room privacy and supervisionKids go in pairs or with parent. Coaches do not enter while children are inside. Adult monitor in corridor for class-day workshops.Low
Aerial riggingAnchor / bolt / karabiner failureDaily pre-class visual + load check by qualified Lead Coach. Annual structural inspection. Rigging log book signed daily.Low
Fall onto hard surfaceCrash mats positioned under every active aerial point before class. Mats not moved during class without coach check.Low
Coach attempting drop they’re not qualified to spotSkill matrix in BSC CRM gates which drops each coach can supervise. Lead Coach signs off complex drops.Low
Pickup / dropoffChild wandering to carpark unsupervisedChildren released only on the studio floor to a known adult. Under-7s formally signed in/out at the office.Low
Unauthorised adult collecting a childAuthorised Collectors list per family in BSC CRM. ID requested if collector is unknown.Low
SupervisionCoach alone with a child in a closed roomDoors stay open. Two-adult rule for off-floor conversations. Camera coverage of main room.Low
Unscreened adult on studio floorVisitors sign in at office. Tradespeople escorted at all times. No visitor on the floor during a children’s class.Low
Online & photoImage of a child posted without consentMedia Consent toggle per family in BSC CRM. Photos checked against the toggle before publication. No personal-device photos.Low
Private messaging between coach and minorAll parent contact routed through the BSC CRM Inbox. Personal channels prohibited.Low
Medical / first aidInjury during classFirst Aid kit on the office wall. At least one HLTAID011-certified person on every shift. CPR refresh annually. 000 protocol on the wall.Low
Anaphylaxis / asthma / seizureMedical notes captured per student in BSC CRM at sign-up. EpiPen / asthma plan kept with the student’s parent during class where applicable.Low
EmergencyFire / smoke in studioPrimary exit: rear roller door to carpark. Secondary: front office door onto Harper St. Monthly evacuation drill. Extinguisher serviced annually.Low
Missing child during classLead Coach roll-counts at intervals. 5-minute search protocol (Doc BSC-CS-12). Parent + 000 if not located.Low
Severe weather (storm / hail) on outdoor sessionOutdoor and offsite sessions check BoM forecast morning-of. Move indoors / cancel if a Severe Weather Warning is current within 60 km.Low

3. Review

  • Weekly: rigging log + first-aid kit check.
  • Monthly: evacuation drill, Lead Coach walkthrough.
  • Quarterly: CRM compliance register review (Blue Card, First Aid expiries).
  • Annually: full Risk Management Plan refresh, signed off by Director.
  • Immediately after any incident: related control reviewed; new controls added if needed.
Approved: Rhett Morrow, Director & Child Safety Officer — 27 May 2026.
