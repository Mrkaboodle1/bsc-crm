1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

BigStar Circus is a children’s circus school. The wellbeing of every child who walks through our doors — or flies overhead — is our first and unbreakable priority.

We are committed to the 10 Queensland Child Safe Standards set by the Queensland Family and Child Commission (QFCC) and to the laws and codes that protect children in Queensland, including the Working with Children (Risk Management and Screening) Act 2000.

Zero tolerance. Any form of abuse, neglect, harm, grooming or unsafe behaviour towards a child will be reported, investigated and acted on without exception. Every member of our team — staff, contractors, volunteers and parents — is responsible for upholding this standard.

1.1 Who this framework applies to

  • All BigStar Circus staff, coaches and trainees
  • All contractors, performers and offsite acts (e.g. balloon twisters, party hosts)
  • All volunteers and work-experience participants
  • All children, teenagers and adult participants attending classes, workshops, performances or events
  • All parents, guardians, carers, NDIS support workers and visitors

2. Safety Culture Statement

At BigStar Circus we believe a strong safety culture is built every day in every interaction. We expect our team to:

  • Speak up the moment something feels unsafe — rigging, behaviour, supervision, anything.
  • Listen to children when they tell us they are uncomfortable, tired, scared or hurt — without dismissing or rationalising it away.
  • Stay visible. Doors stay open, blind corners are walked through, no one-on-one moments in private spaces.
  • Document everything. If it didn’t get logged in the BSC CRM, it didn’t happen.
  • Improve constantly. Near-misses are gold — we use them to fix systems, not blame people.

3. Record Keeping

All compliance records are stored in two layered, redundant locations:

  1. BigStar Circus CRM (app-chi-silk-29.vercel.app) — live operational records: attendance, incidents, complaints, training register, contact data.
  2. Child Safe Standards folder (Rhett’s secure file system — my-assistant/bsc-crm/Child Safe Standards/) — signed policy PDFs, Blue Card scans, First Aid certificates, Certificates of Currency, parent waivers.

Records are retained for a minimum of 7 years after the last interaction with the child or their family, in line with QFCC retention guidance.

4. Child Participation

Children are not bystanders in their own safety. We invite their voice through:

  • The BSC Star Reward System — recognising effort, kindness and bravery as well as skill.
  • End-of-term anonymous "How did your term feel?" cards.
  • An open "tell-a-coach" rule at the start of every class.
  • Showcases and Talent Shows where children choose their own act and music within safety guidelines.

5. Parent Communication

  • New families receive the Parent Handbook (Doc BSC-CS-14) and sign the BigStar Waiver at first booking.
  • Weekly class updates and term plans are sent via email/SMS from the BSC CRM Inbox (with approval from a director before sending).
  • Concerns can be raised in person, by phone (0439 188 179), by email (admin@bigstarcircus.com.au), or via the anonymous Complaint Form on the BSC website.

6. Continuous Improvement

The Master Framework is reviewed:

  • Quarterly — quick internal review by the Child Safety Officer.
  • Annually — full document refresh and policy reissue.
  • Immediately — after any reportable incident, complaint upheld, or change in QFCC standards.
