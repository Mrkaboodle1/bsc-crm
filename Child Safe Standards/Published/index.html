Child Safe Compliance Pack · Index

BSC-CS-01
Master Child Safe Framework Manual
Organisational commitment, the 10 QFCC standards, governance, safety culture, record keeping, continuous improvement.
BSC-CS-02
Staff Handbook
Welcome, values, required Blue Card & First Aid, supervision rules, Star Reward system, cross-discipline skill matrix, disciplinary process.
BSC-CS-03
Child Safety Policy
Zero-tolerance position. Child rights, inclusion, recruitment screening, professional boundaries, mandatory reporting obligations.
BSC-CS-04
Staff Code of Conduct
Expected vs unacceptable behaviour, physical contact rules, grooming prevention, photography, breach consequences. Signed at induction.
BSC-CS-05
Parent Code of Conduct
Respect, pickup procedures, photography/privacy rules, social media expectations, how to raise child-safety concerns.
BSC-CS-06
Risk Management Plan
Full risk register: environment, blind spots, toilets, aerial rigging, pickup/dropoff, online, emergencies, supervision.
BSC-CS-07
Incident Report Form
Printable, fillable form — injury, near-miss, behaviour, child-safety concern. Sign-off sections for reporter, parent, Director.
BSC-CS-08
Complaint Handling Process
Channels (in person, phone, email, anonymous web form). 7-step flow. Confidentiality. No retaliation. Escalation hierarchy.
BSC-CS-09
Child Safe Reporting Flowchart
Visual 7-step "if you suspect harm" flow. Emergency contacts (000, 1300 682 254, 1800 811 810, Kids Helpline). Pin on studio wall.
BSC-CS-10
Staff Induction Checklist
Eight-section checklist: ID, clearances, policies, walkthrough, emergencies, child safety, skill matrix, CRM setup. Three signatures.
BSC-CS-11
Blue Card & Training Register
Master compliance table for every staff member. Blue Card, First Aid, qualifications, internal training log, monthly Director sign-off.
BSC-CS-12
Emergency Response Procedures
Exits: rear roller door + front office door. Injury, fire, missing child, medical event, lockdown, severe weather. Drill record.
BSC-CS-13
Online & Social Media Safety Policy
Student privacy, messaging boundaries (no DMs), photo consent toggle, online classes, BSC vs personal social media, device usage.
BSC-CS-14
Parent Handbook
What to bring, arrival/pickup, communication, Star Rewards, showcases, pricing (Play On, NDIS), child-safety reporting.
BSC-CS-15
Risk Assessment Templates
Six print-ready templates: weekly class, holiday workshop, excursion, performance, aerial activity, community/NDIS group event.
Storing & updating: the live, editable source for this pack lives in my-assistant/bsc-crm/Child Safe Standards/Published/. The published copy is also served from inside the BSC CRM under Compliance so coaches, parents and auditors can download any document instantly. Reviewed annually or after any reportable incident.
