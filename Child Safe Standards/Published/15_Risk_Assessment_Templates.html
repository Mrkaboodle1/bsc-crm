How to use these templates

Before each activity, the Lead Coach completes the matching template. Sign and file in the BSC CRM Compliance log. Re-use a previous assessment only if conditions are identical — otherwise fill a fresh one.

Risk rating: Likelihood (L 1–5) × Consequence (C 1–5). Aim for residual risk ≤ 6 after controls.
