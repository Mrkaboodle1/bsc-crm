Online & Social Media Safety Policy

Where the BigStar brand lives online — and where our coaches absolutely do not.

BigStar Circus Pty Ltd · Version 1.0 · 27 May 2026
BigStar Circus
Online & Social Media Safety

1. Student Privacy

  • Student personal data (name, age, contact, family info, medical notes, NDIS data) is stored only in the BSC CRM. Access is limited to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis.
  • Photos and video of a child are linked to a Media Consent toggle in the BSC CRM. Photos may be used publicly only when the toggle is on.
  • Full names of children under 18 are not published publicly. First name + class only.
  • Children’s schools, suburbs, and routines are never published.

2. Messaging Boundaries

Coaches do not message children one-on-one through any private channel — ever. No Messenger, SMS, WhatsApp, Snapchat, Instagram DM, Discord, TikTok DM, email, or game chat. All parent and student communication is via the BSC CRM Inbox or the BSC email address.

2.1 Approved channels

  • BSC CRM Inbox (admin@bigstarcircus.com.au) — the primary record of every parent/family exchange.
  • BSC SMS sender (admin@) for class reminders and term updates.
  • Phone calls from the BSC numbers (0439 188 179, 0437 186 533) — logged in the CRM the same day.

2.2 What is forbidden

  • Personal phone numbers or social media handles given to a student.
  • Friending / following a student on a personal account.
  • Direct messages with a student or unaccompanied minor on any platform.
  • Group chats containing students set up outside of BSC’s approved channels.

3. Photography & Video Consent

  • Media Consent is captured at sign-up via the Parent Waiver and stored as a toggle on the family record in the BSC CRM.
  • Before any post is published, the team member who took the photo or designed the post checks the toggle for every identifiable child.
  • Photos of children with consent off are deleted within 24 hours; never used internally except for incident records.
  • Parents may withdraw consent at any time by updating their preference with admin@bigstarcircus.com.au.

4. Online Classes (rare)

If a session is delivered online (e.g. a sick coach mentoring a sick student, or a tournament prep call):

  • Always run via the BSC official platform — not personal Zoom / personal device.
  • A second BSC adult joins the call OR the session is recorded.
  • Both parent and student must opt in.
  • Sessions are scheduled through the CRM — not via personal messaging.

5. Social Media Posting (BSC accounts)

  • Only authorised admin (currently Rhett, Jackie) posts to the official BSC Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.
  • Captions are factual, warm, and never identify a child by full name, school, or address.
  • No live-streaming of classes.
  • Posts featuring children are reviewed against Media Consent before scheduling.
  • Direct messages from minors to the BSC accounts are referred to a parent — never engaged with one-on-one.

6. Staff Personal Social Media

  • You may identify as a BigStar coach on your personal accounts. You must not identify specific students, families, addresses, or post images of children in BSC settings without explicit BSC authorisation.
  • Personal opinions that conflict with BSC values (discrimination, child-safety jokes, glamorising drugs/alcohol) reflect on your role at BSC and may be grounds for review.
  • If a parent or student finds you on a personal account — politely decline the follow.

7. Device Usage at the Studio

  • Personal phones in the staff locker during class time.
  • BSC-issued iPad is used for Roll Call, star awards, music, and incident logging.
  • No screens visible to children during class except for class music and the BSC roll-call interface.
  • No filming of students on personal devices for any reason.

8. Reporting Online Concerns

If you become aware of online behaviour that worries you — a coach’s public posts, a student’s wellbeing on social media, a stranger’s contact with a student through BSC channels — tell the Director the same day. Document via the Incident Report Form (Doc BSC-CS-07).

Approved: Rhett Morrow, Director & Child Safety Officer — 27 May 2026.
