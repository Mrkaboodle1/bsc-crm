### Notes for Rhett
- **No ABN → you legally must withhold 47%.** So insist on an ABN before paying as a contractor.
- **GST:** a contractor only adds GST if they're GST-registered (turnover ≥ $75k). Most small/part-time coaches aren't — so usually no GST.
- **Super:** if the contract is "wholly or principally for their labour" (a coach's time), the ATO treats them as an employee FOR SUPER — so you pay super on top. That's the arrangement you described for your new coach. ✅
- **Sham-contracting watch:** if they really work like an employee (your roster, your gear, your direction), they may legally BE an employee. Have your accountant confirm the contractor arrangement is genuine.
