# Junior Employee Onboarding Checklist — Big Star Circus (QLD)

> For your young trainee coaches (under 18) employed as CASUAL JUNIORS. Tick
> every box before their first paid shift. Sources: QLD Business / Office of
> Industrial Relations + Fair Work. Your accountant/bookkeeper confirms pay.

## Eligibility & consent (QLD child-employment law)
- [ ] Child is **13 or older** (QLD minimum working age; 11 only for delivery work).
- [ ] **Written parental/guardian consent** obtained and kept on file (required for under-16s). Refresh if duties/hours change.
- [ ] Hours within limits:
  - School term: **max 4 hrs/school day, 12 hrs/school week**
  - School holidays: **max 8 hrs/non-school day, 38 hrs/week**
  - **No work 10pm–6am**, and never during their school hours.

## Tax & pay
- [ ] **TFN Declaration** completed (get a TFN — free, ~5 min online). No TFN = withhold top-rate tax.
- [ ] **Junior award rate** confirmed for their age (a % of the adult rate — see the pay research). Check the exact figure on Fair Work's Pay Calculator (PACT). **Don't underpay — junior minimums still apply.**
- [ ] Pay basis recorded in CRM (Team → coach → Pay & tax): casual junior, rate, TFN on file.
- [ ] **Super:** NOT required for under-18s working **30 hrs or less per week** — tick "Super paid = no / N/A" in the CRM.
- [ ] Bank account details on file.
- [ ] Set up in Xero for **Single Touch Payroll** (even casual juniors must be reported). Record every payment — no off-the-books cash.

## Safety & compliance
- [ ] **Blue Card** status confirmed (check the requirement for a paid under-18 working with children — confirm with Blue Card Services; don't assume exempt).
- [ ] First Aid awareness / never left solely in charge / always supervised.
- [ ] Read & signed: Staff Code of Conduct + Child Safety Policy (Compliance page).
- [ ] Emergency contact recorded.

## Induction
- [ ] Coach Academy started (the BSC Way, class structure, behaviour, tablet/roll).
- [ ] Read the "New Rules from Term 4" staff briefing (Compliance page).

Your trainees (Louie 15, Aliyah 14, Charlie 13) are **casual junior employees**, not
contractors. The good news: **no super** (under 18, ≤30 hrs), and with a TFN their
small earnings are usually tax-free. The only fix needed: make sure the **rate
