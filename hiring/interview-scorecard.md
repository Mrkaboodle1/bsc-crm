# Coach Interview Scorecard — Big Star Circus

> Use this for every coach interview. Score each area 1–5. The goal isn't the
> most skilled performer — it's the most **coachable, reliable, kid-loving**
> person you can grow into a Head Coach.

---

## The one question that matters most

> **"Do you want to perform, or do you want to build performers?"**

If their eyes light up about *the kids' progress* — green light.
If it's about *their own stage time* — that's your 🚩.

---

## Score each area (1 = no, 5 = excellent)

| # | Area | What good looks like | Score /5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Coaching-first heart** | Talks about kids, progress, patience — not themselves | |
| 2 | **Foundations** | Gymnastics/acro/dance/cheer background, body awareness | |
| 3 | **Great with big energy** | Has real strategies for excitable / ADHD / boundary-testing kids | |
| 4 | **Reliability** | Stable history, shows up, takes it seriously | |
| 5 | **Coachable / moldable** | Open to learning circus skills, takes feedback well | |
| 6 | **Warmth & communication** | Parents and kids will love them | |
| 7 | **Long-term intent** | Wants a career & to grow, not a stop-gap | |
| 8 | **Compliance ready** | Blue Card + First Aid (or willing to get immediately) | |

**Total: ___ / 40** — Strong candidate 30+, worth a trial class 24–29, pass below 24.

---

## Questions to ask

1. Why do you want to coach kids (not just do gymnastics)?
2. Tell me about a child who was really struggling — what did you do?
3. A class is going wild and you've lost them. What's your move?
4. How do you handle a child who won't follow instructions?
5. You don't know circus skills yet — how do you feel about being trained from scratch?
6. Where do you want to be in 2–3 years?
7. What does reliability mean to you?

---

## Always do a paid trial class

References lie; a room full of kids doesn't. Watch for:
- Do the kids **respond** to them?
- Do they **set up structure** (stations, clear rules, little downtime)?
- Do they **stay calm** when it gets chaotic?
- Do they **praise specifically** and often?

---

## 🚩 Red flags
- "I'm a performer looking for steady income between shows"
- Vague or job-hopping history
- Talks over you / not coachable
- Uncomfortable with or dismissive of neurodiverse kids
- No Blue Card and no urgency to get one
