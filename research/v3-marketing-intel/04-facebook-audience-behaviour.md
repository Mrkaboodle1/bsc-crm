# 04 · Facebook Audience Behaviour Report (2026)

*Meta has fundamentally rewired what's possible on Facebook in the last 18 months. Most of the playbook BSC's competitors are using is out of date.*

## The big shift

In June 2025 Meta **consolidated thousands of detailed interest categories** into broader buckets. On **31 March 2026 Meta removed detailed-targeting exclusions entirely** ([Social Media Today coverage](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-removes-more-detailed-ad-targeting-options-facebook-instagram/757856/)). Health, religion, politics and sensitive-attribute categories have been gone since 2022. **Under-18s cannot be targeted with interests, lookalikes, or custom audiences at all** — only age, gender (recently removed for teens), and location.

**Translation:** Old playbooks like "stack Parenting + Bluey + Frozen + Disney + Gymbaroo + Gymnastics" do not work in 2026 the way they did in 2022. They still spend money — but the precision they imply is illusory. **Meta's AI is now the targeting system, not the marketer.**

## The 2026 paid playbook for local kids' businesses

### The 70 / 20 / 10 split

The current consensus on budget allocation for local service businesses on Meta in 2026:

- **70–80% Advantage+ broad** with location pinned to a 15–25 km radius around the studio
- **10–20% retargeting** (website visitors, video viewers >50%, IG engagers, customer-list custom audiences)
- **5–10% lookalike-seeded campaigns** for testing new creative

This is dramatically simpler than the 2020-era playbook of stacked interest ad sets. ([Jon Loomer on Meta Ads Targeting 2026](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-targeting-2026/), [Adligator on Meta Broad Targeting](https://adligator.com/blog/meta-broad-targeting-advantage-plus-audiences-2026).)

### Customer-list lookalikes are the highest-quality cold audience

Upload a CSV of **paid enrolled families** (not trial bookings — paid). Minimum 100 records, ideally 500+. Source location set to Australia (lookalikes can't accept city/radius), then layer a Gold Coast radius in the ad-set's *location* field. Start with 1% lookalike — test 3% and 5% as you scale.

**Implication for BSC right now:** the Tectonic export tomorrow becomes the seed audience. Even 60–80 paid enrolments produces a usable 1% lookalike across Australia, geo-clamped to GC.

### Advantage+ Audience with "audience suggestions"

Per [Vaizle Insights' 2026 guide](https://insights.vaizle.com/facebook-ads-guide/audiences-targeting/), Advantage+ Audience *beats* hand-picked interest stacks for cold acquisition — provided you feed Meta strong directional suggestions: **Parenting + (one of: Gymnastics / Dance / Cirque du Soleil / Children's activities) + behaviour "Engaged shoppers"**, plus the geo-radius. Meta's AI treats the suggestions as hints, not walls, and expands intelligently within the radius.

### Bluey, Frozen, Disney+ interest pages still exist

Despite the consolidation, a few branded-page interests survive. Disney's 2026 Bluey push has kept it as a usable interest signal. Use these for **creative testing** in dedicated small ad sets, not for scaling.

### The geo trap nobody talks about

A common Gold Coast mistake: leaving "People recently in this location" enabled. That puts your ad in front of every Sydney/Melbourne family on a 10-day GC holiday. **Always set "People who live in this location" only.** Saves 15–30% of spend immediately.

### The new account ramp

First 2–4 weeks: use one interest-targeted ad set to build conversion data. Once the pixel has fired 50+ conversions, graduate to Advantage+ broad. Skipping this step throws good money at Meta's AI before it has signal to work with.

## What's working in creative right now

The 2026 algorithmic preference is unambiguous: **UGC-style, vertical, native, selfie-mode** beats polished-commercial by 8.7× engagement in some verticals ([Adligator's Facebook ad creative trends 2026](https://adligator.com/blog/facebook-ad-creative-trends-2026)).

For kids' activity ads specifically, the formats winning on Meta in May 2026:

### Format 1 — Selfie-mode coach intro (15–30 sec)

> *"Hi, I'm Sarah. I've been teaching circus on the Gold Coast for 9 years. Here's the one thing every parent gets wrong before their kid's first class…"*

Vertical phone footage. Coach to camera. Hard-cut at the 8-second mark to B-roll of class. CTA in-frame: free trial booking link.

### Format 2 — Named-fear hook (10–15 sec)

> *"Is your shy kid hiding behind you at every birthday party? This class was built for them."*

Open with the parent's fear named on-screen text overlay. Cut to wide of a shy kid joining the warm-up circle. CTA: *"Free Trial Saturday."* Names the niche — does not try to be everything to everyone.

### Format 3 — Transformation cut (20–30 sec)

Week 1 vs Week 12 split-screen of the same kid. The story is in the cut, not in the voiceover. Music light or absent. Text overlay: *"Twelve weeks, same kid."* Saves more than any other format because parents send it to grandparents.

### Format 4 — Voucher-tied direct response (15 sec)

> *"Got your $200 Play On voucher? You can use it here. Free trial first, voucher auto-applied when you enrol. Expires 12 June."*

This is the format Cirkidz Adelaide is currently running ([Meta Ad Library ID 2061288291101437](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=AU&q=cirkidz)) — no Gold Coast operator I checked has the equivalent.

### Format 5 — Lead-form / "next 10 kids" scarcity

The format Invincible HQ (Wetherill Park NSW) is running ([Meta Ad Library ID 1301503265031499](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=AU)):

> *"ATTN: PARENTS in [SUBURB] — searching for the next 10 kids to join our confidence and discipline program."*

Direct-response lead form. Lower bar than "book a trial" because no calendar pressure. Inbox follow-up converts.

## What this tells us about BSC's competitive position on Meta

**Nobody in BSC's direct radius is running a 2026-grade Meta campaign.** Gold Medal Gymnastics, Southport Gymnastics, Flipz, BOUNCE, Dance Mafia, Inspire Athletics, Hartmann Taekwondo — none have multi-creative Advantage+ campaigns visible in the Ad Library at the time of writing. The dominant local marketing is organic FB posts and the occasional boosted post.

**Implication:** A modest Advantage+ campaign with the 5 formats above and a 20 km Molendinar radius would likely deliver **the highest-quality ad presence in the Gold Coast kids' activity market** — without needing Meta-tier ad spend.

## Hygiene checklist before any BSC paid spend

- [ ] Conversions API installed (server-side pixel events)
- [ ] At least 50 historical conversion events in Meta (use trial bookings if no enrolments yet)
- [ ] Customer-list custom audience built from paid enrolments (post-import tomorrow)
- [ ] "People who live in this location" only — never "Recently in"
- [ ] Excludes: existing customer list (don't re-target enrolled families)
- [ ] Single ad account, not delegated through an agency
- [ ] Budget cap, not lifetime — cap daily spend until creative is dialled in
