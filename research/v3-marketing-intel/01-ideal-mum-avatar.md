# 01 · The Ideal BigStar Circus Mum Avatar

*The canonical BSC customer, drawn from 2026 Australian parent research, BSC's lifecycle data, the Mi3/Mumpower Aussie Mums 2025 report, and inbound enquiry patterns to admin@bigstarcircus.com.au.*

## Meet Erin

**Age:** 36
**Location:** Pimpama, Gold Coast (one of the GC's fastest-growing suburbs — 32.9% of the population is aged 0–14 in nearby Ormeau-East–Stapylton, per the [City of Gold Coast 2026 stats](https://www.goldcoast.qld.gov.au/About-our-city/Population-data/Statistics-for-families-children)).
**Family:** Married, two kids — Maeve (8) and Tommy (5).
**Day job:** Part-time admin at a Brisbane logistics firm, three days a week. Drops the kids at before-school care, picks up after.
**Household income:** ~$110–130k combined.
**Phone usage:** ~3.5 hours/day, ~70% of that on Instagram and Facebook.

## Her week

| Day | What's on |

| Mon | Tommy at OSHC, Maeve at gymnastics trial (didn't love it) |

She drives ~250km/week shuttling between school, work, activities and her parents' place in Helensvale.

## Her relationship with her kids' activities

- Maeve tried **ballet** at 5 (cried at the recital), then **gymnastics** at 7 (lasted one term — *"the coach kept yelling at the older girls"*). Tommy has done two terms of swim and a "messy play" session.
- Erin has spent ~$4,200 on kids' activities in the last 18 months. She can quote the cost of every program off the top of her head.
- She is **not** looking for the next Olympic gymnast. She wants both kids to find a place where they **belong** and that gives Maeve a confidence injection before high school.
- She's quietly worried Maeve might be "a bit anxious" but hasn't said it out loud. She's googled *"shy kid activities"* twice this month.

## Her real fears (the ones she doesn't post about)

1. *"My kid will be the only one who can't do the thing."*
2. *"I'll spend $400 on a term and Maeve will hate it after week 2."*
3. *"Tommy is on iPads way too much. I feel guilty every day."*
4. *"What if there's a creepy coach? How do I really know?"*
5. *"What if I pick the wrong activity and waste the Play On voucher?"*

These map directly to the AIHW data showing **13.9% of Australian children aged 4–17 meet criteria for a mental disorder**, with anxiety the #1 disorder in girls (7.7%) — [Young Minds Matter](https://youngmindsmatter.thekids.org.au/our-research/prevalence-of-mental-disorders/). The Triple P 2024 National Parenting Survey confirms **two-thirds of parents feel weekly guilt about time spent with their child**.

## How she makes a buying decision

Per the [Mi3 / Mumpower 2025 Aussie Mums Report](https://www.mi-3.com.au/23-04-2025/trust-and-reviews-shape-australian-mums-buying-decisions-reveals-new-report):

- **50% consult another mother before purchasing**
- **1 in 4 most recent purchases triggered by a trusted recommendation**
- **6 in 10 actively promote brands they favour online** — once she's in, she'll bring three friends.

Erin will research a kids' activity for **5–14 days** before booking a trial. In that window she will:

1. Visit the website 2–3 times across different devices.
2. Stalk the Instagram for ~12 minutes total.
3. Look at Google reviews.
4. Ask in one mum group ("anyone tried X for shy kids?").
5. Maybe DM the business with a softball question to "feel them out".
6. Book the trial — usually 7–10 days after first awareness.

She rarely calls. She always DMs first.

## What converts her

The Allianz 2024 youth-sport study found **88% of parents say the top motivators for their kids are being cheered on, positive affirmation, and feeling their support network is proud** — [source](https://www.allianz.com.au/about-us/media-hub/aussie-kids-drop-out-of-sport.html). For Erin, the words that hit:

- *"You don't need to be flexible, coordinated, or confident to start."*
- *"Every kid finds their thing — we'll help you find your kid's."*
- *"No rankings, just applause."*
- *"Half our families are NDIS or Thriving Kids. We don't talk down to your child."*
- *"$200 Play On voucher applied at checkout."*

She's repelled by:

- Hard sells ("BOOK NOW LIMITED SPOTS!")
- "Elite squad" or "competitive team" framing
- Generic stock photos (she can smell them)
- Watermarked-and-recycled content
- Promises that sound like marketing not mums

## What kid she's bringing

**Maeve (8)** — middle of the bell curve coordination-wise. Reads 2 books a week. Loves making up dances in her room with the door shut. Has one tight best friend. Cried the first time she did a forward roll in front of the gymnastics class.

**Tommy (5)** — climbs literally everything. Cannot stand still. Has been suspended from kindy once for biting. Best afternoon of his life was when his grandma let him roll down a hill 40 times.

Both kids together is the ideal: BSC's multi-discipline model + no-rankings ethos works for shy Maeve AND chaotic Tommy in the same class — without humiliating either of them.

## What she'd say at the school gate that turns into a sale for BSC

> *"I took the kids to that circus place in Molendinar last Saturday for a free trial. Maeve was nervous but the coach got her doing tricks within ten minutes — she came home and showed her grandparents on FaceTime. They're properly into it. And the coach just gets the shy ones. We're signing up for a term."*

That's the **one sentence we want every BSC family saying within 60 days of joining.** Everything in the customer journey (doc 15) is engineered to produce that sentence.
