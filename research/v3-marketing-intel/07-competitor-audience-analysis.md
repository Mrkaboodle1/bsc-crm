## Audience-style breakdown

Looking at *who* each competitor's content speaks to — based on follower engagement, content style, and visible enrolment profiles:

### The "competitive squad" cluster

**Inspire Athletics**, **Gold Coast All Stars Cheer**, **Southport Gymnastics**. Their audience: parents of athletically inclined kids willing to commit to elite-track training. **High parent investment** (multiple sessions/week, travel for comps, uniforms, fees). The kid who washes out of this cluster is BSC's natural inbound.

### The "national chain" cluster

**BOUNCE**, **GKR Karate**. Their audience: parents looking for cheap, convenient, drop-and-leave activities. Volume-driven. Low-loyalty. **BSC's positioning is the opposite** — small, relationship-driven, premium-feel. No need to compete on price.

### The "family business" cluster

**Gold Medal Gymnastics**, **Hartmann Taekwondo**. Their audience: parents wanting warmth + tradition. Multi-generational family loyalty. **BSC overlaps here** — same warmth, but with multi-discipline variety and explicit neurodivergent welcoming.

### The "dance / drama / performance" cluster

**Dance Mafia**, **Helen O'Grady**. Their audience: parents (mostly mums) wanting their kids in performance pursuits. Heavily-girl-skewed. **BSC's circus catches both**: the kid who loves drama (BSC's drama discipline) and the kid who loves movement (acro/aerial). Plus boys feel welcome at circus in a way they often don't at dance.

### The "extreme sports" cluster

**Flipz Academy**. Their audience: parents of high-energy/risk-tolerant kids, especially boys. **Limited overlap with BSC's positioning** — BSC offers risk-managed performance, not extreme-sport tricks.

## The single biggest gap in the market

**Nobody on the Gold Coast owns the "creative + physical + nurturing + neurodivergent-welcoming" positioning.** This is BSC's whitespace. The doc 09 industry trends doc shows this is a growing segment per AIHW + UWA + NDIS data — and the only competitor running creative this way is Flipside Circus, who's 75+ km north.

## Geographic concentration of competition

Plotting competitors on a map:

- **Southport / Labrador / Ashmore**: Southport Gym, Helen O'Grady, GKR Karate — high density of mid-tier competition
- **Burleigh / Robina / Mermaid**: BOUNCE, Hartmann (Merrimac), various dance — south GC competition
- **Northern corridor (Coomera / Pimpama / Ormeau)**: **mostly empty** — GC All Stars Cheer (Ormeau) is the only direct studio. **This is BSC's biggest unserved geographic pool.**
- **Molendinar itself**: Gold Medal Gymnastics is literally in the same industrial complex as BSC

## Competitor advertising spend signals

Of the 12 competitors above:

- **None** appear to be running 2026-grade Advantage+ campaigns on the Gold Coast at the time of this analysis
- **BOUNCE** runs national paid spend but it's brand-level, not local
- **Most are relying on organic FB posts + occasional boosted posts** — the equivalent of 2019 marketing

**Implication:** A well-executed BSC paid campaign would be the highest-quality ad presence in the Gold Coast kids' activity market with **modest spend** (~$1k-$3k/month).
