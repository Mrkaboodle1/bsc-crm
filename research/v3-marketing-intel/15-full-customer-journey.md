# 15 · The Full BSC Customer Journey

*From "never heard of BSC" to "tells five friends." Five stages, the psychology at each stage, what BSC needs to do, and the systems Jacky already runs (or will run).*

## The five stages

```
1. AWARENESS  →  2. INTEREST  →  3. TRIAL  →  4. ENROLMENT  →  5. ADVOCACY
   (Days 0-3)     (Days 1-14)     (Day of)    (Term 1-2)      (Month 3+)
```

Each stage is a different mental state. Each requires a different message, channel, and system.

## Stage 1 — Awareness (Days 0-3)

### What's happening in the parent's head

She's not looking for BSC. She's living her life. BSC appears in her feed, mentioned in a mum group, or via a school newsletter. **She files it as a name to consider later.**

### What she sees

- A coach-led Reel on her IG (most common)
- A friend tags her ("@jenna check this out for Maeve")
- A school P&C bulletin mentions BSC's holiday programme
- A Gold Coast Mums Group thread mentions BSC
- A Kids on the Coast article references it

### What she thinks

*"Huh, that looks interesting. I'll come back to it."*

### What BSC needs to do

- **Be everywhere she might encounter you.** That means consistent IG presence (doc 05), Meta paid (doc 04), and intentional presence in the discovery channels (doc 06).
- **Don't sell at awareness.** The job here is to be memorable, warm, and specific. Not to convert.
- **The hook is named, not generic.** *"Is your shy kid hiding behind you at every birthday party?"* > *"Sign up for circus today!"*

### Systems

- **Server-Jacky** classifies inbound mentions if they come via email
- **Marketing/compose** publishes regular content
- **IG / Meta paid creative** runs continuously

### Metric

Reach (impressions). Doesn't matter how many click — at awareness, you're measuring how many are flicker-aware of BSC.

## Stage 2 — Interest (Days 1-14)

### What's happening in the parent's head

She's now actively considering whether BSC is for her family. The doc 10 buying-journey kicks in: background research, trust-signal hunt, social validation, soft commitment.

### What she does

1. Visits the BSC website 2-3 times across different devices
2. Scrolls the BSC IG for ~12 minutes total
3. Reads 4-5 Google reviews
4. Maybe asks in the GC Mums Group: *"Has anyone tried BigStar Circus?"*
5. DMs BSC with a softball question — *"Hi, what age does aerial start?"*

### What she thinks (decision tree)

```
Does the website look professional? → No → CLOSED
                                    ↓ Yes
Can I see who the coaches are?    → No → CLOSED  
                                    ↓ Yes
Are there real reviews?           → No → STALLED
                                    ↓ Yes
Is the pricing transparent?       → No → STALLED
                                    ↓ Yes
Does the reply to my DM come fast → No → COLD
   and feel human?                  ↓ Yes
                                  → BOOKS TRIAL
```

### What BSC needs to do

- **The website passes the trust audit** (doc 14)
- **Coach pages exist** with Blue Card mention, tenure, photo
- **Pricing visible** in dollars per week
- **Recent IG content** with named hooks (doc 05)
- **Reply to her DM within 60 minutes** — Jacky drafts, Rhett approves on phone

### Systems

- **Jacky inbox triage** (every 15 min) catches her DM/email if she contacts admin@
- **Approval queue (/inbox)** surfaces the draft to Rhett
- **Resend + ClickSend** send the approved reply within minutes
- **Jacky chat (/jacky)** lets Rhett ask Jacky to draft a longer custom reply if needed

### Metric

DM-to-trial-booking conversion %. Target: 50%+. (Industry average for kids' activities with poor DM ops: 15-25%.)

## Stage 3 — Trial (Day of)

### What's happening in the parent's head

She's anxious. She's brought her kid (or kids) on a Saturday morning. She wants the trial to work. She also wants to feel confident saying no if it doesn't. **She's making a final decision in about 90 minutes.**

### What she does

- Arrives 10-15 minutes early
- Stands awkwardly while the kid clings to her leg
- Watches the trial class from the side
- Has 4-5 quick interactions with coaches / other parents
- Talks to her kid in the car on the way home
- Decides — usually within 24 hours — whether to enrol

### What she's watching for

- Does the coach learn the kid's name immediately?
- Is the kid SMILING by minute 10? (the single best predictor)
- Are other kids welcoming? Or clique-y?
- Is the parent area comfortable?
- Does the coach update her at pickup with a SPECIFIC moment? (not generic "great session!")
- Is the follow-up message warm and not salesy?

### What BSC needs to do (the trial choreography)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| Booking | Friendly confirmation email + arrival info |
| Day before | SMS reminder: "See you tomorrow! Wear comfy clothes. Park around the back." |
| On arrival | Greeter knows her name, walks her in, makes her welcome |
| Class start | Coach learns kid's name within 2 minutes |
| Mid-class | Kid wins a small thing publicly ("Maeve's got it!") |
| Class end | Coach pulls parent aside, says ONE specific moment ("Maeve was a leader in warm-up — first time in a while a new kid has done that") |
| Departure | "No pressure — go think about it. Term starts in 2 weeks. We have 3 spots in the Saturday class." |
| 2 hours later | SMS: "Hope the kids had fun! Any questions, just text me back." |
| Day 2 | If she hasn't replied, soft email: "Wanted to send Maeve this photo from yesterday." (parents who get photos of THEIR kid convert near-deterministically) |
| Day 4 | If still no reply, one final personal SMS from Rhett: "Hi Erin — no pressure, just wanted to check if we can answer anything. Hope to see you in the family." |

### What BSC must NOT do

- ❌ Hard sell at the end of the trial ("So are you enrolling or what?")
- ❌ Send a generic "Thanks for your trial" auto-email
- ❌ Lose track of who came for trial
- ❌ Leave the parent unattended in the waiting area
- ❌ Allow a senior kid to embarrass the trial kid in any way

### Systems

- **Trial booking flow** (booking system → CRM family record)
- **Server-Jacky** can be configured to send SMS reminders the day before
- **Approval queue** holds the day-of follow-up
- **Family detail page** shows what happened during their trial

### Metric

Trial-to-enrolment conversion %. Target: 60%+. (Industry average: 30-40%.)

## Stage 4 — Enrolment (Term 1-2)

### What's happening in the parent's head

She's committed. She's now watching whether BSC delivers on the trial promise. **The biggest single risk is week 3-4 of term 1**, when honeymoon ends and real schedule + cost reality bites.

### What she experiences

- Drop-off and pickup 1-2× per week
- Probably one missed week due to illness
- A direct-debit payment going through
- The kid coming home, sometimes excited, sometimes tired
- Other parents at the studio (potential new friendships)

### Retention pillars at work

This is where doc 13's four retention pillars do their work:

1. The kid loves it → coach rotation, discipline variety
2. Real friendships → stable class composition, friend pairs, buddy system
3. Coach knows them → daily greeting by name, 60-sec pickup ritual
4. Visible progress → star ledger, termly skill check-in email

### What BSC needs to do

- **Week 4 pulse check** — every family, every term — a single Google Form, takes 2 minutes for the parent
- **Coach signs personal end-of-term email** with one specific kid moment
- **Mid-term photo/video** of the kid (with consent) sent to the parent
- **End-of-term showcase** — opt-in, low-pressure, family-only audience
- **Pause/hold-fee option** prominently offered before any cancel button

### What BSC must NOT do

- ❌ Generic "your payment failed" auto-email — must be personal SMS from Rhett
- ❌ Mid-term silence (no contact between weeks 1 and end)
- ❌ Class change announcements that come cold
- ❌ "We're closed next Friday" notifications that read as last-minute

### Systems

- **Subscription / billing** sync (Stripe, when wired)
- **Family detail page** tracks comms, attendance, billing status
- **Jacky chat** can draft week-4 check-ins per family

### Metric

Term-to-term retention %. Target: 80%+. End-of-year tenure: 18+ months average.

## Stage 5 — Advocacy (Month 3+)

### What's happening in the parent's head

She's now an advocate. She talks about BSC at the school gate, at family dinners, in mum groups. Per [Mi3](https://www.mi-3.com.au/23-04-2025/trust-and-reviews-shape-australian-mums-buying-decisions-reveals-new-report) — 6 in 10 Australian mums actively promote brands they favour online.

### What she does

- Posts about her kid's circus achievement on her own IG (which BSC can re-share with permission)
- Replies to other parents' "anyone tried X?" questions in mum groups
- Refers friends — sometimes specific ("Talk to my Erin, her son does it"), sometimes general
- Buys merch / signs up for second sibling / books a birthday party
- Brings the grandparents to the showcase

### What BSC needs to do

- **Make sharing easy** — a "share to your story" button on every BSC IG post, a referral-credit system
- **Re-share parent content** (with permission) on the official BSC IG — multiplies the social proof
- **Birthday parties + holiday programmes** as continued engagement opportunities for advocates
- **Alumni outreach** — once a family pauses or leaves, they're still in the advocacy chain if treated well

### Systems

- **Referral tracking** (in CRM) — who referred whom
- **Star ledger** (gamified progression for the kids)
- **Stripe / payments** running smoothly so advocates don't get billing pain
- **Marketing / Bulk send** for term-end campaigns

### Metric

Referral % of new bookings. Target: 30%+. NPS: 60+.
