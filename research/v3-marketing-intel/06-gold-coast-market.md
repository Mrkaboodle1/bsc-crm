# 06 · Gold Coast Family Market Research

*The catchment in numbers: ~110,000 kids 0-14, growing fast, concentrated in the northern corridor.*

## Population baseline

Sources: [City of Gold Coast — Statistics for families & children](https://www.goldcoast.qld.gov.au/About-our-city/Population-data/Statistics-for-families-children), [Population Australia 2026](https://www.population.net.au/gold-coast-population/), [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/cities/australia/gold-coast).

- **Gold Coast 2026 population:** ~741,000–762,000 (6th-largest Australian city, fastest-growing of the top 10)
- **Kids aged 0–14:** **109,930** (17.6% of population)
  - 0–4 yrs: 33,491 (5.4%)
  - 5–9 yrs: 37,539 (6.0%)
  - **10–14 yrs: 38,900 (6.2%)**
- **Growth:** **+9,075 more kids 0–14 vs 5 years ago** — the cohort is expanding
- **Families with children (couples):** **68,226** (41.9% of all GC families)
- **One-parent families with children:** **27,956** (17.2%)

## Income reality

This is the variable that quietly determines whether your messaging lands:

- **45.1% of one-parent GC families earn under $800/week** ($41,600/year) — for these families, the **Play On voucher ($200) is materially relevant**, not nice-to-have
- Median dual-income GC family ~$110–130k household (typical for the demographic in suburbs like Pimpama, Coomera, Robina)
- The **Triple P 2024 National Parenting Survey**: 9 in 10 Australian parents have reduced spending due to cost of living; **2 in 5 GC families are actively considering withdrawing kids from activities** (UNICEF Australia 2024)

**Implication:** Pricing transparency matters. *"$28/week — and the first 4 weeks are voucher-applied if you have a Play On code"* converts better than *"Term fees apply"*.

| Suburb | % kids 0–14 | Why it matters |

| **Ormeau (East) – Stapylton** | **32.9%** | Northern corridor, ~20 min from BSC |
| Pimpama | (high, ~30%) | Newest growth area, very young families |
| Coomera | (high) | Sister suburb to Upper Coomera |
| Helensvale | (above-average) | Theme-park-adjacent family suburb |
| Worongary | (above-average) | Inland family belt |

**Translation:** The **northern corridor (Pimpama → Coomera → Upper Coomera → Ormeau)** is the largest single concentration of BSC's target market. Molendinar is a 15–25 minute drive south. **This is the geo-target for BSC's first paid campaigns.**

### Homeschool community hubs

QLD homeschool numbers are growing fast post-COVID. The major hubs:

- **Queensland Homeschool Community** — FB group, ~10,000 members
- **Gold Coast Homeschool Community** — FB group, the GC-specific hub
- Niche GC groups: Gold Coast Christian Homeschoolers, GC Home Educators Buy Swap Sell, GC Homeschoolers, GC Homeschool Unschool Living Tribe, GC Teen Homeschoolers, Homeschool Events & Fun ([source list](https://howdoihomeschool.com/homeschooling-groups-queensland-australia/))
- **HEA Australia local groups** ([hea.edu.au/local](https://www.hea.edu.au/local))

**The opportunity:** BSC already runs weekday homeschool sessions. Flipz Academy is the only competitor with a comparable program, and they don't market it well. **One well-placed post in 4-5 of these groups can fill a term.**

## Demographic-driven catchment strategy

If we segment BSC's addressable market by who lives where + what they need:

| Segment | Where | What pulls them | Friction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northern corridor families | Pimpama / Coomera / Ormeau | Multi-discipline, sibling discount, Sat morning | 25-min drive |
| Inner GC working parents | Southport / Labrador / Ashmore | After-school, school holiday programmes | Time-pressure |
| Hinterland families | Worongary / Mudgeeraba | NDIS-friendly, low-pressure positioning | Distance |
| South GC | Burleigh / Palm Beach | Birthday parties (already do trips for venues) | Distance for weekly |
| Brisbane south | Logan / Springwood / Daisy Hill | Brisbane-Kids editorial coverage + cheaper than Flipside | Need a compelling reason to skip Flipside |

## Play On! voucher — the QLD overlay

[qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/playon](https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/playon):

- Every QLD child 5–17 gets **$200/year**
- **Round 2 opened 29 Jan 2026, applications close 12 June 2026, vouchers must be presented by 19 June 2026**
- **Covers circus, dance, gymnastics + all standard sports** — BSC qualifies if registered
- Provider registration: [qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/playon/providers/register](https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/sports/funding/playon/providers/register)

**Discovery channels for the voucher:** Queensland Savers, school newsletters, provider FB posts, mums' group word-of-mouth. **No GC operator I checked is running paid Meta creative tied to the voucher** — Cirkidz Adelaide is, but they're 1500 km away.
