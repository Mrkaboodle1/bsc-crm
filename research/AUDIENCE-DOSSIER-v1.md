## 0. TL;DR (read this even if nothing else)

- **You need ~6 net-new families/month** for 7 months = ~40 net subs (assuming low churn). At a 50% trial-to-enrol conversion that's **~80–100 trials between now and December.**
- **Your strongest competitive moat is geography + positioning.** Flipside Circus is the nonprofit gold standard, but they're in **Brisbane** (Hamilton, 1hr away). On the GC, there's NO direct equivalent. Aerial Angels (Burleigh) is aerial-only; HAPY Tiny Circus (Currumbin) is toddlers-only; Puzzle Dust is workshops/parties, not weekly classes. **BSC is the only Gold Coast multi-discipline circus school for kids 5–17.**
- **Three strategic windows open right now:**
    1. **Thriving Kids (NDIS replacement)** — launches 1 Oct 2026, procurement starts **May 2026 (now).** If BSC registers as a community provider, autism + developmental-delay kids 0–8 with low-to-moderate needs get funded by the state to come here. This is the single biggest 6-month opportunity in this dossier.
    2. **Play On! Sports Vouchers** — Every QLD kid 5–17 gets **$200/year** of state money to spend at a registered activity provider. BSC qualifies. Most parents have unspent vouchers expiring **19 Jun 2026.** That's a 5-week ad window with built-in financial incentive.
    3. **Reactivation of "past" families** — Lowest cost-per-acquisition is ALWAYS people who already know you. Your CRM currently shows 1 "past" family; the real number is higher. We'll surface them in a focused campaign.
- **The audience is dual-mode** — public marketing speaks to *every* family with the *"fun + belonging"* hook. The deeper mission (welcoming kids the sport system fails) shows up in the photos + stories, **never the headline.**

## 1. Market context

### Australia
- Circus as a children's wellbeing intervention has **growing academic backing** — circus training boosts confidence + emotional regulation 7× more than standard PE ([NIH scoping review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10003779/)). UQ's Health & Wellbeing Centre for Research Innovation is running a current *Performing Arts Experience Questionnaire* on this (you got the email; we should sign BSC up — free third-party validation we can quote in ads).
- **Autism diagnoses are rising and largely fuelled by NDIS** — a UWA article (Apr 2026) confirms the trend ([source](https://www.uwa.edu.au/news/article/2026/april/autism-diagnoses-are-up-largely-fuelled-by-ndis-what-happens-next-isnt-clear)). Families are actively seeking inclusive, non-clinical activities for these kids.
- **Homeschool numbers are growing** post-COVID. Brisbane has dedicated homeschool extracurricular operators (BEXD Create, etc.). BSC is one of the few **circus**-specific homeschool offerings in QLD.

### Gold Coast (BSC's primary catchment)
- **Population: ~741,661** (2026 est.) — Australia's 6th-largest city, fastest-growing of the top 10.
- **41.9% of families are couples with children** — that's our parents.
- Median family age tracks around 39 — sweet spot for kids 5–15.
- **GC parents value low-stress, weatherproof, take-the-effort-away activities.** From the kids' birthday party scan: every venue article highlights "no risk of being rained out", "reserved tables + food + parking", "host included". That's the bar.

## 3. The strategic windows (opportunities to chase NOW)

### 3.1 Thriving Kids — register as a community provider before procurement closes

- **What it is:** A federal program replacing NDIS for kids 0–8 with developmental delay or autism with *low-to-moderate* support needs. Begins 1 Oct 2026, full rollout 1 Jan 2028.
- **Why it matters:** Once registered, BSC becomes a state-funded option for thousands of families currently leaving NDIS. Families won't even need a diagnosis to access funding — that removes the single biggest friction in the autism-services pipeline.
- **What we need to do this month:**
    1. Find QLD's procurement portal (likely [health.gov.au/thriving-kids](https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/thriving-kids) and the QLD Department of Child Safety, Seniors and Disability Services).
    2. Submit the EOI when it opens (May 2026).
    3. Prepare BSC's Inclusive Practice statement, blue card + first-aid evidence, child safety policies.
- **My role:** I'll draft the EOI + Inclusive Practice statement for Rhett to sign off. Bring it to Friday.

### 3.2 Play On! voucher push — 5-week window

- Every QLD kid 5–17 has **$200 of state money** sitting unspent. **19 June 2026** is the deadline to use this round.
- Most families forget. Most providers don't actively chase voucher kids.
- **Campaign idea: "Spend your $200 on something they'll actually love."**
    - Single FB/IG ad set, 5-week run
    - Lead magnet: "Find out if your kid is eligible — 30 sec quiz"
    - CTA: free trial booking, voucher applied at first invoice
- **Target audience:** GC parents of 5–12yos who haven't enrolled their kid anywhere yet this year.

### 3.3 Reactivate "past" families

- 18 families in the CRM right now have a `past` lifecycle stage (or will once we backfill the data).
- They know you, they liked you enough to enrol once, they have low CAC.
- **Campaign idea: "We miss you — come back, first 2 weeks on us."**
    - Personal email from Rhett (not a marketing blast)
    - Phone call from me (when I'm on a server)
    - Drip: email week 1 → SMS week 2 → DM week 3

### 3.4 Sibling discount push (you're under-leveraging this)

- From the data: of 18 sample families, **3 are siblings (Bennett ×2, Edwards ×2, Iyer ×3).** That's 17% multi-kid families today.
- Industry benchmark for kids' activities: 30-35% are multi-kid families.
- **Lift potential:** if every active family enrolled a sibling, you'd hit 100 subs without any new families. **THIS IS THE FASTEST PATH.**
- Campaign: 10% off second sibling, 20% off third. End-of-term reminder to every single-kid family.

## 4. Parent personas

These are composite — built from BSC's actual lifecycle data + market signal + email tone of the parents already enquiring.

### Persona 1 — **Sarah the Searching Mum** *(35-42, GC, 1-2 kids 5-9)*
- **Backstory:** Her son tried soccer, hated it. Her daughter did ballet, cried at the recital. She's looking for something where they can be HAPPY, not pressured.
- **Where she lives online:** Gold Coast Parents FB group, Kids on the Coast magazine, Brisbane Kids, the GC mum Instagram comment threads.
- **What she searches:** *"kids activities Gold Coast no pressure" · "circus for kids" · "alternative to gymnastics"*
- **Her objection:** "Is my kid coordinated enough? What if they're shy?"
- **The line that converts her:** *"You don't need to be flexible, coordinated, or confident to start. We'll meet your kid where they are."*

### Persona 2 — **Carlos the NDIS Dad** *(35-50, GC/Logan/Brisbane, autism or developmental-delay child 5-12)*
- **Backstory:** Has a plan-managed NDIS budget for "skills development" or "community participation". His son has tried 4 OTs, hated 3 of them. Wants something his kid will RUN to.
- **Where he lives online:** NDIS provider FB groups, AuDHD Australia, Yellow Ladybugs, local autism support FB groups, school newsletters.
- **What he searches:** *"NDIS circus" · "autism friendly activities Gold Coast" · "kids social skills NDIS"*
- **His objection:** "Will the instructors get my kid? Or will it be another place we get politely asked to leave?"
- **The line that converts him:** *"Every coach on our floor has worked with neurodiverse kids. Half of our families are NDIS or Thriving Kids. We don't talk down to your child."*

### Persona 3 — **Priya the Homeschool Mum** *(32-45, multiple kids 5-14, often religious or alternative-education families)*
- **Backstory:** Homeschools 2-3 kids. Wants weekday daytime extracurricular that's social + physical, where her kids meet other homeschool kids.
- **Where she lives online:** *Homeschooling QLD* FB group, *Australian Christian Home Schooling*, *Charlotte Mason Home Educators Australia*, local co-op WhatsApp groups.
- **What she searches:** *"homeschool circus" · "homeschool physical activity Brisbane" · "co-op classes Gold Coast"*
- **Her objection:** "Will it fit a Wednesday morning? Sibling discount? Can my 5yo and 11yo go in the same class?"
- **The line that converts her:** *"Wednesday 9:30 / 10:30 / 11:30 — 3 hours of circus across the disciplines, designed for homeschool families. Siblings welcome. Bring snacks, we'll bring the silks."*

### Persona 4 — **Karen the Birthday Mum** *(30-45, hosting a kid's party for 8-15 kids)*
- **Backstory:** Doesn't want a bouncy castle (done it). Wants something memorable. Read articles about "best Gold Coast party venues" and is comparing 4-5.
- **Where she lives online:** Pinterest birthday boards, *Kids on the Coast* magazine, *Inside Gold Coast* lists, recommendation threads in mum FB groups.
- **What she searches:** *"circus birthday party Gold Coast" · "unique kids party ideas Gold Coast" · "birthday parties [age]"*
- **Her objection:** "How much, what's included, is it stressful for the parent? Do I need to bring anything?"
- **The line that converts her:** *"$X per child, $Y minimum. We bring the music, the props, the coach, the photo opportunities. You bring the cake. Done in 90 min."*
- **Benchmark pricing from competitors:** $20-39/child (Hijinx $39, Holey Moley $35, Stunt Park $59, Parties at the Park $20). BSC should sit at **$35-45/child** — premium because it's a real skill, not just play.

### Persona 5 — **Grandma Helen** *(60-75, GC grandparent of 5-12yo grandkids)*
- **Backstory:** Wants to "do something special with the grandkids" during school holidays. Has disposable income. Wants stories to tell her friends. Loves performance + nostalgia.
- **Where she lives online:** Local community FB pages, Probus groups, GC Council "Things to do with grandkids" pages, *Seniors Card QLD* newsletters.
- **What she searches:** *"things to do with grandkids Gold Coast school holidays" · "kids classes school holidays" · "circus holiday program"*

- **The line that converts her:** *"Grandparents' favourite — drop off at 9am, pick up at 3pm, we feed them, we tire them out, they go home with a story."*

### Persona 6 — **Trial-Curious Tara** *(30-40, kid age 4-7, hasn't booked yet)*

## 6. Messaging — DO and DON'T

### DO
- **"No rankings, just applause."** Best 4 words in this whole doc.
- *"Open. Inclusive. No competition. No pressure."*
- *"Every kid finds their thing here."*
- *"We're a safe place for kids the sports system fails."* (Internal — never in copy directly, but it informs everything)
- Show a kid mid-flop in photos — not a perfect pose
- Quote real parents: *"My daughter cried at her last ballet recital. She doesn't cry at BSC."*

### DON'T
- *"Elite athletes"* / *"high performance"* / *"competition team"* / *"national level"* — even when describing trainees. Call them *"BigStar Trainees"* — that's your own word.
- *"Special needs circus"* — ghetto-izes the brand. Inclusivity shows in photos, not labels.
- Stock photos of perfect kids doing perfect poses. Use **only real BSC kids**, only real moments.
- Pricing on a page without booking link nearby — friction kills.

- 50% trial-to-enrol = 40 new subs
- **CAC ≈ $67/family** — well within reason given ~$1,400/year LTV

**Path C — Thriving Kids state contract**

- A delivers +16 subs in 60 days
- B delivers +40 subs over 90 days
